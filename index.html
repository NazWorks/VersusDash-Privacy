VersusDash Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

VersusDash does not collect personal information. The app has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, purchases, chat, or user-generated content.

Local multiplayer

VersusDash uses local-network access so players can join a race hosted by another device or a dedicated game server. Race messages contain temporary player slots, movement input, course state, and results. They do not contain names, email addresses, precise location, advertising identifiers, or contacts.

Information stored on your device

If a player chooses to save a server address, that address is stored locally on the device so the player can reconnect. NazWorks does not receive it. Removing the app removes this locally stored information.

Children

VersusDash is designed for family play. Because the app does not collect personal information, NazWorks does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Players should only join servers operated by a parent, guardian, or other trusted person.

TestFlight and platform services

When a tester installs VersusDash through Apple TestFlight or sends TestFlight feedback, Apple may process device, diagnostic, and feedback information under Apple's own privacy policy. NazWorks receives only the information Apple makes available to the developer for testing and crash diagnosis.

Changes

If a future VersusDash release begins collecting information, this policy will be updated before that release and will explain the information, purpose, retention, and choices available to players.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to feedback@naz.works.
